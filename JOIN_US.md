# Join the MYSverse Development Team!

Welcome to the **MYSverse Development Team**. We are a passionate, collaborative, and volunteer-based team building the future of Roblox and gaming communities.

While we are a volunteer team, we strongly believe that your time, effort, and skills are incredibly valuable. To recognize and support our developers, we built **DevHub**—our custom developer operations dashboard that translates your contributions into real-world rewards, professional physical gear, and career milestones.

Here is why you should join us and how our platform supports you every step of the way:

---

## 1. Guaranteed Task Payouts: The Points Payout System (PPT)

Every task you pick up on our **Linear** workspace has a DevHub complexity level from 1-5. Linear stores those as Fibonacci estimates (`1, 2, 3, 5, 8`), which DevHub maps back to levels (`1, 2, 3, 4, 5`). When you complete a task labeled `PPT` (Points Payout System), you are guaranteed a direct payout based on the normalized complexity level.

- **Choose Your Currency:** Get paid in **Malaysian Ringgit (MYR)** or **Robux** depending on your preference.
- **Highly Competitive Rates:**
  - **RM20.00** per complexity level (e.g., level 3 = **RM60.00**, level 5/XL = **RM100.00**).
  - **1,200 Robux** per complexity level (e.g., level 3 = **3,600 Robux**, level 5/XL = **6,000 Robux**).
- **Automatic Weekly Approvals:** Completed PPT tasks are automatically compiled, processed, and paid out weekly through our integrated providers (Billplz, Xendit, FinSys).
- **Professional Accounting:** View and download transaction slips as PDFs directly from your dashboard for easy record-keeping.

## 2. Weekly & Lifetime Performance Incentives

On top of your standard PPT earnings, our active developer incentive program rewards consistency, throughput, and long-term commitment:

- **Weekly Throughput Rewards:** Complete 5 or more qualifying tasks in a single week to instantly secure an extra **RM30.00 / 1,800 Robux** throughput bonus.
- **Active-Day Kickers:** Get rewarded just for showing up. Meeting our daily activity targets adds an extra kicker to your weekly payout.
- **Streak Bonuses:** Consistency pays off. Hit your weekly throughput targets consecutive weeks in a row to stack up additional streak rewards.
- **Lifetime Milestones:** Celebrate your growth and achievements with lump-sum payouts when you cross lifetime completed task counts:
  - **25 Tasks Completed:** **RM40.00 / 2,400 Robux** bonus.
  - **50 Tasks Completed:** **RM75.00 / 4,500 Robux** bonus.
  - **100 Tasks Completed:** **RM150.00 / 9,000 Robux** bonus.

## 3. Discretionary Monthly Bonuses

Not all important work can be labeled with a simple PPT estimate. Bug fixing, refactoring, code reviews, assisting other team members, and maintaining documentation are crucial to our success.

- **Automatic Candidate Syncing:** All non-PPT Linear tasks assigned to you are synced automatically as "Bonus Candidates."
- **Monthly Reviews:** Admins review your contributions and approve a grouped monthly **BONUS** transaction paid directly to you based on your completed tasks, at a rate of up to **RM10.00 / 500 Robux** per estimation point.
- **In-App Alerts:** Receive real-time push and email notifications showing your potential bonus earnings (`Up to X`) so you know exactly where you stand.
